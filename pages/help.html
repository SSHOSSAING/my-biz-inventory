Role Overview
The system has 3 roles. Each person logs in with a 4-digit PIN and sees only what they are allowed to access.
Owner
Full access to everything. Google Sync, Finance, Investors, Reports, Settings, and all staff data.
Manager
Dashboard, Products, Invoice, and Restock. Can see purchase prices. Max 20% discount.
Salesman
Invoice page only. Cannot see purchase prices. Max 10% discount. Serves by: name on invoice.

Owner — Full System Guide
As owner you have access to everything. Here is what you should do and when.
Daily Tasks
1
Check the Dashboard every morning — review yesterday's sales, outstanding dues, and low stock alerts.
2
Go to Finance → Due Collection — follow up on customers with outstanding balances and record any payments received.
3
At end of day, go to Settings → Push to Sheets to back up all data to Google Sheets. This is your cloud backup.
4
Check Settings → Staff Activity Log to review what your manager and salesmen did today.
Weekly / Monthly Tasks
1
Open Reports — review revenue, gross profit, net profit, and top selling products for the month.
2
Open Finance → Cash Flow — see the monthly revenue vs expenses vs profit chart.
3
Open Investors & Partners — calculate profit distribution for the month and record any withdrawals or profit shares paid out.
4
Go to Settings → Export JSON Backup — save a full backup file to your computer or USB once a month.
Managing Staff Accounts
1
Go to Settings → Staff & Access Control to add new accounts. Enter the staff name, select their role (Manager or Salesman), and set a 4-digit PIN.
2
To change anyone's PIN — click the 🔑 key button next to their name. You must enter your own owner PIN to authorise the change.
3
To remove a staff member — click the remove button next to their name. They immediately lose access.
4
The Activity Log shows every sale, stock entry, and login by each staff member with timestamp. Use this to verify work.
Important: Only the Owner account can Push/Pull Google Sheets, change settings, view finance & investor data, and access reports. Keep your owner PIN private — do not share it with staff.
Manager — Your Daily Tasks
You can access Dashboard, Products, Invoice, and Restock. Here is how to use each one.
Dashboard
  • Check today's total sales amount
  • See which products are running low on stock
  • View recent sales and restock entries
  • See outstanding customer dues (but cannot collect — tell the owner)
Invoice / POS
  • Create sales for customers
  • Add multiple products to one invoice
  • Apply discounts — maximum 20% of subtotal
  • Print, download PDF, or send via WhatsApp
  • Select payment method (Cash, bKash, Nagad, etc.)
Products
  • Add new products with name, category, price, and stock
  • Edit existing products (prices, stock levels)
  • Import products in bulk from a CSV or Excel file
  • Manage product categories
  • Export the product list to CSV
Restock
  • Record stock received from a supplier
  • Select product, enter quantity and purchase price
  • Click Post Stock — stock is added immediately
  • Use Quick Restock buttons for low-stock items
  • Add or edit suppliers using the person+ button
You cannot: delete posted sales or stock entries, access Finance/Reports/Settings, push or pull Google Sheets data, or give discounts above 20%. If you need any of these, ask the owner.
Salesman — How to Create a Sale
You only have access to the Invoice page. Follow these steps for every sale.
1
Open the app → Login screen
Select the Salesman tab → select your name from the dropdown → enter your 4-digit PIN → tap Login.
2
Invoice opens automatically
A new invoice is ready. The Invoice No is auto-generated. Today's date is already filled in.
3
Enter customer details (optional)
Select a customer from the dropdown, or type their name and mobile number directly. For walk-in customers, you can leave this blank.
4
Add products
Select a product from the Product dropdown — the selling price fills automatically. Enter the quantity. Click Add. Repeat for each item. The system will warn you if stock is insufficient.
5
Apply discount (if permitted)
You can give a discount of maximum 10% of the subtotal. If you enter more, it will be automatically reduced. If a customer asks for more discount, they must speak with the manager or owner.
6
Select payment method and enter amount paid
Choose Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Card. Enter the amount the customer gives you. The system shows the change to return, or the due amount if they pay partially.
7
Click Post Sale
The invoice is saved, stock is deducted, and your name appears on the invoice as Served by. You can then Print, Download PDF, or Send via WhatsApp.
8
Click New Invoice for the next customer
The form clears and a new invoice number is generated. You are ready for the next sale.
Remember: You cannot see product purchase prices. You cannot edit or delete a sale once posted. You cannot give more than 10% discount. If you make a mistake on a posted sale, tell the manager or owner.
Tip: Your session stays active for 8 hours. If you close the browser and reopen it, you will need to log in again. This keeps customer and price data safe.
Google Sheets Sync — Complete Setup Guide
Sync lets you back up all data to Google Sheets and access it from any device. Only the Owner can do this.
Internet required. The app works fully offline without sync. Sync is only needed when you want to back up data to the cloud or access it from another device.
Create a Google Cloud Project
1
Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Gmail you want to use for sync.
2
Click the project dropdown at the top → New Project → Name it my-biz-inventory → Click Create.
3
In the left sidebar go to APIs & Services → Library. Search for Google Sheets API → Click it → Click Enable. Then search for Google Drive API → Enable that too.
Set Up OAuth Consent Screen
4
Go to APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen → Select External → Click Create.
5
Fill in: App name = My Biz Inventory, Support email = your Gmail. Click Save and Continue through the next 3 screens without changes.
6
On the Test users step — click Add Users and enter your Gmail address. Click Save. (While in Testing mode, only listed users can connect.)
Create OAuth Client ID
7
Go to APIs & Services → Credentials → Create Credentials → OAuth client ID. Set Application type = Web application.
8
Under Authorized JavaScript origins, click Add URI and add your app URL. If hosted on GitHub Pages: https://yourusername.github.io. For local testing: http://localhost:8080. Do NOT add a trailing slash.
9
Click Create. A popup shows your Client ID — it looks like: 123456789-abc.apps.googleusercontent.com. Copy it.
Add Client ID to the App
10
Open assets/js/sheet-sync.js in any text editor. Find: CLIENT_ID: '<<YOUR_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID>>' and replace with your actual Client ID. Save the file.
11
Upload the updated sheet-sync.js to GitHub (if hosted) — go to your repo → navigate to the file → click the pencil icon → paste your updated code → Commit changes.
Connect and Create Your Spreadsheet
12
Log in as Owner → go to Settings → Google Sheets Integration → click Connect Google. A Google login popup appears — sign in and allow access.
13
Click Create Spreadsheet. The app creates a Google Sheet in your Drive named [Business Name] Inventory Database with 10 tabs automatically set up.
14
Click Push to Sheets — all your local data uploads. A success message shows how many records were synced. Check Google Drive to verify.
Daily Sync Workflow
Shop Counter
Post sales all day
End of Day
Owner clicks Push
Owner's Phone / Other Device
Log in → Settings → Pull from Sheets
Rule of thumb: One device pushes. Other devices pull. Never have two devices pushing at the same time — this can create duplicate rows in your sheet.
